Timeline

StarCraft: Ghost ran from a 2002 announcement to a 2014 cancellation, with a development build frozen in 2004 and released in 2019. This page lists the dated milestones. Dates marked as a month only carry the source's precision.

DateEvent
1999 Nihilistic Software is founded by former LucasArts developers, including Ray Gresko and Robert Huebner.
2002-09-20 Blizzard announces StarCraft: Ghost at the Tokyo Game Show. It is described as a tactical-action console game with Nova, psi powers, and Calldown strikes, co-developed with Nihilistic and co-published in Japan by Capcom. The target is late 2003. Announcement press release.
2002 The Win32 Tokyo Game Show demo (Star.exe family) is compiled. It is the older of the two executable families in the leak.
2003-05 Blizzard publishes an E3 2003 page and press release for the game. E3 2003 press release.
2003-09 A Tokyo Game Show 2003 press release restates the feature set. Multiplayer is added to the design during 2003.
2004-05-08 The Xbox development build is compiled. This is the artifact released in 2019. Hidden Palace record.
2004-06 Blizzard announces a delay and that Nihilistic is no longer on the project. Development moves to Swingin' Ape Studios. GameSpot report.
2005-05 Blizzard acquires Swingin' Ape Studios. Wikipedia summary.
2005-11 The GameCube version is cancelled; the game is left on PlayStation 2 and Xbox. Eurogamer report.
2005 BlizzCon 2005 shows a multiplayer build. This is the Swingin' Ape era, which the leaked build predates.
2006-03 Blizzard places the game on indefinite hold and refocuses its console team. Game Developer report, GamesIndustry.biz.
2014 Blizzard confirms the game is cancelled. Wikipedia summary.
2019-12-29 Finn Hillbilly dumps and releases the May 8 2004 Xbox prototype, according to the Hidden Palace record.
2020-02-16 The build spreads publicly; an Archive.org item is uploaded and the press covers the leak. Polygon, The Verge.
2022 Kevin Manthei self-releases the Nihilistic-era original soundtrack. VGMdb entry.

Last reviewed October 2026. Corrections welcome through the repository that hosts this wiki.
