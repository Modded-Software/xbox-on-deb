Campaign

The leaked build exposes six playable mission segments and one disabled mission. The same build's string tables preserve the full intended slate, roughly 45 mission IDs across seven acts, most of them never built or left disabled. The campaign plan is far larger than the playable content.

Playable missions

These mission segments are enabled in the build's chronicle file. Names and internal identifiers come from the build itself.

Disabled mission

The Borgo Refinery (1_3_REFINERY) is present but commented out in the chronicle file. It can be re-enabled.

The planned slate

The build's general string table carries roughly 45 mission identifiers across seven acts. The unbuilt and disabled slate includes three complete arcs that do not appear in playable content.

ArcMission names preserved
Protoss arc In the Path of Titans, Sacred Ground, Khaydarin Core, Arbiter Tribunal, Artemis Boss.
Zerg arc The Shadow Hunters, The Way of the Warrior, Terrazine Mining A, B, and C, The Path of Shadow.
Endgame bosses Aries Boss, Hauler Boss, Vulcan Boss.
StarCraft 1 homage act Desperate Alliance, Revolution, Backwater Station, The Trump Card, The Jacobs Installation, The Invasion of Aiur, The New Gettysberg.

The Protoss missions are named in the strings but have no enabled implementation. Their unit and creature assets exist, which places them in the "content-rich, wiring-poor" category described in the gap analysis.

Unfinished content in the strings

The string tables still contain placeholders, which shows the slate was being shaped rather than finished. The following are quoted from the build's text tables.

  • Objective placeholders: "Find the MacGuffin part #1", "Find the Flux Capacitor", "Find the Heisenberg Compensator", "Find the Minovsky Particles", "Find the Maltese Falcon".
  • Temporary objectives marked "[TEMP]", including "Disable Heisenberg Compensators", "Flux Capacitor", and "Deactivate laser wall".
  • A briefing line that reads "And here comes a Zergling Rush!!! (Yeah, I know you can't see it. But it's coming. Someday.)".
  • Joke dialogue for an infested marine, written as "...kill me, I'm infested blah blah blah...".

What the campaign does not include

The marketed single-player length of 12 to 15 hours is not met. The playable content is a few hours at most, across six segments of a 45-segment plan. There is no Protoss play and no full act structure in the artifact. The multiplayer promised in the official FAQ is absent, too.
