StarCraft: Ghost

StarCraft: Ghost was a stealth-action console game announced by Blizzard in 2002 and cancelled in 2014. It was built by Nihilistic Software, rebuilt by Swingin' Ape Studios, and never released. This wiki documents its development, the May 8 2004 development build that leaked in 2020, and the gap between the announced feature set and what the build contains.

Quick facts

Status
Cancelled in 2014; on indefinite hold from March 2006
Announced
September 20 2002, Tokyo Game Show
Developers
Blizzard, Nihilistic Software, Swingin' Ape Studios
Planned platforms
PlayStation 2, Xbox, GameCube (GameCube dropped in 2005)
Leaked build
Xbox, compiled May 8 2004, dumped and released by Finn Hillbilly
Playable content
Six mission segments from a planned slate of about 45
