People and studios

StarCraft: Ghost was made by Blizzard Entertainment with two external studios, Nihilistic Software and Swingin' Ape Studios. This page lists the organizations and the people named in public sources, with the role each is known for.

Studios

StudioRoleNotes
Blizzard Entertainment Publisher and brand owner Owned the StarCraft series and oversaw the project from announcement to cancellation.
Nihilistic Software First developer, 2002 to June 2004 Founded in 1999 by former LucasArts developers. Built the 2004 build and the 2002 Win32 demo.
Swingin' Ape Studios Second developer, 2004 to 2005 Known for Metal Arms: Glitch in the System. Rebuilt the game around multiplayer. Acquired by Blizzard in May 2005.
Capcom Japanese co-publisher Named in the 2002 announcement.

Interviews

Two interviews carry useful detail. Longtime Blizzard fan site Blizzplanet interviewed Nihilistic's Robert Huebner about the studio's difficulties, and interviewed sound designer Julian Kwasneski about the game's audio. Polygon's 2016 postmortem gathered nine developers for a full account of the project's collapse.
