Story and setting

StarCraft: Ghost is set four years after the events of Brood War. The story follows Nova Terra, a Terran Ghost drawn into Project Shadow Blade, a secret program that created unstable psionic operatives called Spectres. The campaign was cancelled long before the plot was finished, so parts of this account come from the marketing and later StarCraft games.

Nova Terra

Nova is a Ghost, a Terran operative with psionic training and cloaking technology. The game's official story page describes a backstory in which her family dies and she is taken into the Ghost program. She is the single playable protagonist in the leaked build, and a development profile named after her unlocks every mission.

Project Shadow Blade and the Spectres

Project Shadow Blade is a covert Terran program that produces Spectres, operatives enhanced with terrazine gas. The enhancement grants powerful psionic abilities and leaves many subjects mentally unstable. Nova's mission set puts her in conflict with the program and its creations. The Protoss and Zerg arcs in the planned campaign were meant to widen the conflict beyond the Terran conspiracy.

Playable classes named in the multiplayer plan, the Ghost, Light Infantry, Marine, and Firebat, are also part of the setting rather than the single-player plot.

Later canon

Elements of the StarCraft: Ghost story were reused in StarCraft 2. Nova Terra, Project Shadow Blade, terrazine, and the Spectres appear in later games, which is why several plot details can be described even though the original campaign was never released. Treat those details as later canon rather than as content recovered from the build.
