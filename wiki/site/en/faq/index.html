FAQ

Short answers to the questions readers ask most, each pointing to the page or source that carries the detail.

What was StarCraft: Ghost?

A stealth-action console game set in the StarCraft universe. Blizzard announced it in 2002, and the player would control the Ghost operative Nova Terra. It was never released.

Was it ever cancelled?

Blizzard put the game on indefinite hold in March 2006 and confirmed the cancellation in 2014. See the timeline.

Can I play it?

Not officially. A development build leaked and runs in the Cxbx-Reloaded emulator, and the hosting repository documents how. This wiki does not host the build. See the leak.

How much of the game is playable in the build?

Six mission segments, from a planned slate of about 45. No multiplayer, no Protoss campaign, and about a few hours of content. See the campaign page.

Does the build have multiplayer?

No. The 16-player class-based mode was built later by Swingin' Ape Studios, after the build's May 2004 date. See multiplayer.

Which platforms was it planned for?

PlayStation 2, Xbox, and GameCube. The GameCube version was cancelled in November 2005. A PC version was never planned.

Who leaked the build?

Hidden Palace credits Finn Hillbilly with dumping and releasing the May 8 2004 Xbox prototype, with a file release date of December 29 2019. The build spread widely in February 2020.

Is the leaked build legal to download?

It is unreleased, copyrighted material. This wiki does not host or link to the game files. It documents the build from public sources.

What engine does it use?

NOD, the engine from Nihilistic's earlier game Vampire: The Masquerade - Redemption. An engine configuration file named masquerade.ini survives in the build.

Why was it cancelled?

Public accounts point to an unresolved design conflict between a squad-based game and a single-hero stealth game, a change of studio, and the success of World of Warcraft pulling staff and resources. See development.

Did any of it survive into other games?

Yes. Nova Terra, Project Shadow Blade, terrazine, and the Spectres appear in later StarCraft titles. See story and setting.

Where can I see the concept art and hear the soundtrack?

The media page links to published concept art collections and to the self-released soundtrack.
